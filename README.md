MIMIC-III Admissions & Mortality Analysis

This project processes and analyzes patient admission records from the MIMIC-III dataset by:
 > Cleaning and merging admissions and patient demographic data
 > Calculating hospital length of stay (LOS)
 > Estimating patient age at admission
 > Identifying in-hospital and 30-day post-discharge mortality
 > Generating descriptive statistics
 > Producing publication-quality visualizations for exploratory analysis
The workflow was designed to provide insight into how admission type, age, and demographic factors relate to hospitalization outcomes.
